⚔️ Wizardry: Classic Quadrilogy (I to IV) & 13 Apple II Scenarios

React 18 / TypeScript Three.js / WebGL Python (UCSD Extractor) Reverse Engineering (Pascal / 6502 ASM) 3D WebGL Gaming

Faithful, modernized recreation of the seminal Sir-Tech RPG saga (full Quadrilogy I–IV + 9 Apple II community campaigns). Complete reverse engineering from vintage Apple II floppy disk images and original UCSD Pascal / 6502 assembly source:

  • Real-time Three.js 3D engine: 1st person dungeon view, dynamic torch lighting, distance fog, retro wireframe mode (1981 Apple II).
  • Interactive tactical minimap (Key M): live floor exploration on vector SVG with tactile D-Pad and touch gestures.
  • Authentic game rules simulation: attributes, spell tables, party management, per-coordinate body recovery expeditions.
  • NTSC color decoding: original Apple II monster portraits and ambient soundtrack (Kentaro Haneda).
  • Python automated disk parser: parses .dsk images and multi-platform packaging (Web / Android Capacitor).
